## AI for public education

As AI tech lead, I lead the team that designs, builds and rolls out AI and data systems with state education departments. The work began in Himachal Pradesh and is expanding to multiple states. These systems have to hold up with non-technical users, several languages, limited connectivity and high-stakes institutional decisions.

| 21st → 2nd | 66,000 | 199 | 4,700+ |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Himachal Pradesh's national rank in Grade 3, NAS 2021 → PARAKH 2024, while I built the state's data analysis and school-level reporting | Government teachers reached through state assessment, training and reporting systems | PM SHRI schools supported through digital workflows | School heads supported through leadership and training workflows |

- **School-level analysis for PARAKH 2024.** Regular data analysis and school-level reports that guided academic interventions ahead of the national learning-outcomes survey. The state also placed 3rd among states in Grades 6 and 9.
- **[SAARTHI](https://saarthi.hp-projectprerna.com/), a teacher competency management system.** AI-enabled, NPST-aligned assessment and professional growth: self and peer assessment, classroom observation, student feedback, teacher portfolios and AI-supported recommendations.
- **Samarth and Manthan.** A training and certification platform, and a mobile-first app for teacher diagnostics and field data capture.
- **TLM Lab Portal and Lesson Plan Portal.** Searchable, curriculum-linked teaching resources and lesson plans for teachers.
