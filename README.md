## Building systems that work beyond the demo

I work at the intersection of **technology, public policy, education, and field implementation**. My role often begins before a product has a specification and continues after it reaches users: clarifying the problem, translating policy into workflows, designing the architecture, guiding delivery, and turning implementation data into decisions.

My focus is not technology in isolation. It is building systems that remain usable under real-world conditions—non-technical users, multilingual environments, limited connectivity, complex reporting hierarchies, and high-stakes institutional decisions.

## Selected system work

### [SAARTHI — Teacher Competency Management System](https://saarthi.hp-projectprerna.com/)

An AI-enabled platform for National Professional Standards for Teachers (NPST)-aligned assessment, professional growth, and evidence-based decision-making.

- Designed product architecture, assessment workflows, evidence models, and role-based user journeys
- Designed dashboards and workflows for self-assessment, peer assessment, classroom observation, student feedback, and teacher portfolios
- Structured AI-supported recommendations, professional-growth tracking, mentoring, and leadership workflows
- Supported technical implementation, rollout planning, and continuous product improvement

### Samarth — Training and Certification Platform

A centralized system for structured teacher training, participation monitoring, certification, and training analytics.

- Designed the platform architecture, training workflows, certification logic, and administrative dashboards
- Defined reporting structures and supported coordination across technical, academic, and field teams

### Manthan — Teacher Assessment App

A mobile-first application for teacher diagnostics, field data capture, and Training Needs Assessment workflows.

- Designed mobile assessment journeys, data models, and competency-wise capture flows
- Reviewed application performance and refined usability for field teams and non-technical users

### TLM Lab Portal

A digital extension of Teaching-Learning Material Labs for resource discovery, teacher co-creation, innovation sharing, and continuous academic support.

- Led portal architecture, resource structuring, UX direction, content organization, and technical implementation

### Lesson Plan Portal

A searchable repository of curriculum-linked, classroom-ready lesson plans, teacher guidebooks, and TLM-integrated resources.

- Designed the repository, content model, search and navigation, and teacher-facing workflows
